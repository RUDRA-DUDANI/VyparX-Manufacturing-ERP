Your information and choices

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how VyaparX ERP (“VyaparX”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit our website, register for an account, or use the ERP service. Please also read our Terms & Conditions.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to website visitors, account owners, employees and other people whose information is entered into a VyaparX company workspace. A company administrator controls access to that workspace. If your employer or another organization invited you, ask that organization about its handling of business records it enters into VyaparX.

Depending on the context, VyaparX may handle account and website information for its own service operations and may process company records on behalf of the organization that uses the service.

2. Information we handle

  • Registration and account details: name, email address, phone number, password credential handled by Firebase Authentication, and account and role identifiers.
  • Company profile details: company and registered business names, business address, country, state, postal code, GSTIN if provided, business type, industry, employee range, location and selected business requirements.
  • Workspace records: information entered into CRM, sales, purchasing, inventory, production, transport, accounting, tasks and related modules. These records may include information about your organization’s customers, suppliers, employees, orders, financial activity and business operations.
  • Files: images and PDF documents that an authorized user chooses to upload, such as invoices and other business documents.
  • Service and preference data: role and access changes, record history and status updates, and limited browser storage used for preferences, synchronization and the optional remembered-email feature.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, authenticate users, associate users with their company, apply role-based access, provide ERP features, save and display workspace records, process workflow actions, store uploaded files, respond to support requests, send service emails when configured, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Welcome, task and account emails are sent through configured providers. If email delivery is unavailable, account registration and saved workspace records are not rolled back.

4. Service providers and sharing

To operate the service, information may be processed by providers configured for the deployment, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for accounts and business records, Cloudinary for uploaded files, and Resend for service emails. Public pages also load some fonts and scripts from third-party content delivery services. Those providers may receive connection information such as IP address, browser and device details when your browser requests their resources. Their own privacy terms also apply.

We do not sell workspace records. Information may be available to authorized users in the same company workspace according to their assigned roles. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect the service and its users, or as part of a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

5. Storage, security and international processing

Workspace data is stored in company-scoped cloud records, and uploaded files are stored with the configured file provider. We use authentication and server-side access checks designed to restrict records to authorized company users. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; keep credentials confidential and notify your company administrator if you suspect unauthorized access.

Cloud providers may process or store information in locations determined by the deployment and their infrastructure. Contact us if you need details about the providers configured for your workspace.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and company information is retained while needed to operate the account and service. The organization using VyaparX is responsible for deciding how long its operational records should be kept. Some delete actions in the application mark records as deleted rather than immediately erasing them from all systems. Records may remain where needed for security, backups, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

To request access, correction or deletion of your account information, email us from the registered address. If the request concerns a company workspace, contact its administrator as well; we may need the administrator’s authorization before changing organization-controlled records.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can keep your registration details accurate, manage browser preferences, and ask your company administrator to update or remove workspace information. Where applicable law gives you additional rights, you may contact us to exercise them or raise a privacy concern. We may ask for information needed to verify and route a request.

8. Children

VyaparX is a business operations service for organizations and their authorized personnel, and is not designed for children to create personal accounts.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or applicable requirements change. The latest version and effective date will be posted on this page. Material changes will be communicated through the service or by email when appropriate.
