VYPARX BUSINESS WORKFLOW

One Connected Business Workflow.

From first inquiry to final payment. One complete flow.

01 CRM

Every business relationship starts here.

Capture customer inquiries, understand requirements, and provide customized quotations seamlessly to kickstart operations.

  • Inquiry Tracking
  • Requirement Gathering
  • Custom Quotations
  • Status Updates
Workflow Label: Inquiry → Requirement → Quotation
02 SALES ORDER

Turn customer approval into action.

Once a customer approves a quotation, VyparX converts the approved requirement into a structured Sales Order. Every product, quantity, price, delivery commitment, and payment condition becomes available to the teams responsible for execution.

  • Confirmed Sales Orders
  • Complete Order Visibility
  • Execution Trigger
  • Connected Operations
Workflow Label: Quotation Approved → Sales Order → Planning → Execution
03 PRODUCTION

Turn orders into finished products.

VyparX connects confirmed customer orders with production planning, material requirements, manufacturing activities, and quality verification so teams know what to produce, when to produce it, and what needs to be completed.

  • Production Planning
  • Material Requirements
  • Production Tracking
  • Quality Control
Workflow Label: Sales Order → Production Plan → Manufacturing → QC → Finished Goods
04 PURCHASE & INVENTORY

Know what you have. Buy what you need.

VyparX keeps inventory connected with production and purchasing. Teams can identify shortages before production stops, raise purchase requirements, receive materials, and keep stock records updated in one continuous workflow.

  • Live Stock Visibility
  • Material Shortage Detection
  • Purchase Workflow
  • Inventory Synchronization
Workflow Label: Material Requirement → Stock Check → Shortage → Purchase → Material Received
05 TRANSPORT

Move every order to the right destination.

When finished goods are ready, VyparX connects dispatch planning with vehicle assignment, driver allocation, delivery tracking, and proof of delivery so every shipment can move with clear responsibility and visibility.

  • Dispatch Request
  • Vehicle & Driver Assignment
  • Delivery Planning
  • Proof of Delivery
Workflow Label: Ready for Dispatch → Vehicle + Driver → Dispatch → Delivery
06 ACCOUNTING

Turn every business activity into financial clarity.

VyparX brings sales, purchases, production expenses, transport costs, payroll, invoices, and payments together so management can understand the complete financial impact of daily business operations.

  • Sales Accounting
  • Purchase & Expense Accounting
  • Payroll Integration
  • Financial Reporting
Workflow Label: Sales + Purchase + Production + Transport + HR → Accounting
07 PAYMENT

Close the financial loop.

Once an invoice is generated, VyparX keeps payment information connected to the original transaction. Teams can clearly see what has been paid, what is pending, and what still needs attention.

  • Invoice Generation
  • Payment Tracking
  • Outstanding Visibility
  • Transaction Completion
Workflow Label: Invoice → Payment Due → Payment Received → Reconciliation
08 ORDER COMPLETED

From first inquiry to final payment. One complete flow.

VyparX connects every stage of the business journey so an order never gets lost between departments. From the first customer inquiry to production, inventory, transport, accounting, and final payment, everything remains connected and traceable.

  • One Source of Truth
  • Complete Traceability
  • Department Coordination
  • Management Visibility
Workflow Label: CRM → Sales Order → Production → Purchase & Inventory → Transport → Accounting → Payment
